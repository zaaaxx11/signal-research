## 7. Honest limits

1. **`verified_columns_count == filled_columns_count` after P6.** Every
   surviving cell has been through the rule table for its domain; a cell no
   rule constrains (e.g. `name`) is recorded as "not contradicted", not as
   "independently corroborated". The alternative — reporting `0` — is the
   fabricated-verification claim Phase 3 removed, and the wiring test forbids
   both. This is a definition, not a measurement, and it is stated here.
2. **V2/V3 only act on the `bars` series.** A quote-only row carries no bars,
   so both rules are no-ops for it; the fixtures exercise the series shape P2
   will fill.
3. **V9 uses 30 bp of spot for equities / 15 bp for indices**, the 04 §2
   tightening, not the looser 2%-of-spot of 02 §3.7. Both numbers are named
   constants and only the tighter one is tested.
4. **V13's off-session close is `session_date T20:00Z`.** The row carries no
   exchange calendar; the request's `max_staleness_s` wins whenever it is set,
   which is every production job (default 900 s).
5. **V8 checks the call convention on `opt_delta_atm`** (`0 < Δ < 1`); a put
   delta is not distinguishable from the row alone, so a negative value is
   rejected rather than guessed.
